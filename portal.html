How it Works

  1. Create an account using your college email.
  2. Post an item you've found or lost.
  3. Browse the board or search for your item.
  4. Contact the other person to return the item safely.
  5. Mark the item as claimed once it's returned.

Community Guidelines

  • Only post genuine lost or found items.
  • Use your college email.
  • Do not post fake listings.
  • Meet in safe public places while returning items.
  • Respect everyone's privacy.
